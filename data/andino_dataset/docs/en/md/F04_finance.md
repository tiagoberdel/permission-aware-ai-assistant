## Detail
Combustibles del Plata: payment term 45 days, balance at September UYU 9.8 million. Neumáticos Uruguay: 30 days, UYU 3.1 million. Taller Mecánico Cordón: 60 days, UYU 1.4 million. The supplier Seguros Rioplatense charges in advance, with no term.

## Payment management
- Payments are scheduled by due date, prioritizing fuel and insurance because of their impact on operations.
- Invoices are paid only with an approved purchase order and the requesting area's sign-off.
- Payments are concentrated in two windows per month to keep cash organized.

## Observations
It was agreed with Combustibles del Plata to keep the current term during the peak season. Invoice differences are resolved together with the purchasing area before the due date.

## Payment calendar
Payments are concentrated in two windows per month. Due dates that fall between windows are brought forward to the earlier window, unless agreed otherwise with the supplier. Finance prepares the payment list in advance and submits it to the finance management for approval.

## Balance reconciliation
Each quarter Finance reconciles balances with the main suppliers and requests written confirmation. Differences are investigated and corrected before the accounting close.

## Supplier portfolio risks
Dependence on the fuel supplier and the concentration of tire purchases with a single supplier are the main risks. Alternatives are evaluated to maintain competitive conditions and ensure supply.

## Recording
Invoices are recorded in the system with their due date and the associated purchase order. Invoices without an approved purchase order are held until regularized.
