# IT Frequently Asked Questions

*Andino Logística S.A. | Document G11 | Date: 2026-08-12*

## Passwords and access
**How do I reset my password?** Go to clave.andino.com with your corporate username and follow the steps; you will receive a code by text message.

**What do I do if I lose my token?** Notify the help desk.

**What if the code does not arrive?** Check that your mobile number is up to date in the self-service portal; if the problem persists, call the help desk.

## Equipment and email
**How do I request new equipment?** Your direct manager submits the request through the IT portal, stating the reason.

**Can I use my personal phone for company email?** Yes, with two-step authentication, but without saving attachments that contain customer data.

## Tickets

Tickets are answered within a maximum of 24 working hours. Security incidents are reported following the systems use policy.

## Printers and connectivity
**How do I connect my device to the floor printer?** Select your floor's printer from the corporate list; if it does not appear, ask the help desk to add it.

**Why is the VPN not working?** Check your internet connection and that your device is up to date. If the error continues, note the message that appears and report it to the help desk.

## Video calls
**Which tool do I use for customer meetings?** The corporate video-call tool. For external meetings the invitation is sent in advance and audio is checked before starting.

## Software
**Can I install a program I need?** Request it through the IT portal stating what you need it for; IT assesses the security and the license before installing it.

## Email
**How do I set up my email signature?** Use the corporate template available in the IT portal. The signature includes name, position, area and the corresponding extension.
