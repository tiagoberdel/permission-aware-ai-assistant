# Medical Certificate Procedure

*Andino Logística S.A. | Document H08 | Date: 2026-05-22*

## Submitting the certificate
Medical certificates are submitted to HR within 48 hours of the start of the absence. They can be handed in on paper or sent by email to HR from a corporate or personal address.

## Filing
They are filed in the folder H-LM with restricted access. Only authorized HR staff access the certificates, and they are kept for the legal retention period.

## Extended absences
Absences of more than 3 days are reported to the health insurance provider. HR coordinates with the direct manager the coverage of the position during the absence and, when appropriate, the return-to-work plan with the employee.

## Confidentiality
The diagnosis is not disclosed to direct managers. Direct managers are only told the start and end dates of the absence and whether it was justified.

## Absences without a certificate
Absences not justified within the deadline are handled under the attendance policy. Exceptions for force majeure are assessed by HR case by case.
