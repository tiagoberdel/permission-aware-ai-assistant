## Central reception
The central reception is open Monday to Friday from 8 to 17. Outside those hours, calls are forwarded to the warehouse guard post.

## How to use this directory
- For questions about pay, leave or certificates, call HR (extension 4130).
- For supplier payments and travel allowance reimbursements, call Finance.
- For quotes and customer complaints, call Sales.
- For incidents in the warehouse, notify the shift supervisor first and then Operations.
- For access or equipment problems, call the IT help desk.

Extensions are updated whenever the structure of the departments changes.
