## Project Zafiro
Tender from a pharmacy chain for nationwide distribution, estimated value USD 2.4 million, probability 35%. It requires a cold chain for part of the products and daily deliveries in the interior. The proposal is submitted with Operations' support for route design.

## Project Ámbar
Reverse logistics contract for a retailer, USD 0.6 million, probability 60%. It includes pickup of returns at stores, sorting at the warehouse and restocking or destruction as the case may be.

## Confidentiality of information
The conditions of both offers are reserved until they are awarded. The pipeline is reviewed every 15 days at the sales meeting and updated in the CRM on the same day as each stage change.
