# Current Rate List (September 2026)

*Andino Logística S.A. | Document V02 | Date: 2026-09-01*

## Rates per pallet
List rates per pallet, in force since 1 September 2026, for general cargo:

| Route | Rate per pallet |
|---|---|
| Montevideo–Rivera | UYU 4,250 |
| Montevideo–Salto | UYU 5,100 |
| Montevideo–Punta del Este | UYU 2,900 |

## Surcharges
Refrigerated cargo: a 22% surcharge on the list rate of the route. Urgent orders with less than 48 hours' notice are quoted case by case with the sales manager.

## General conditions
Rates do not include VAT and are updated quarterly according to the fuel index. Discounts on these rates follow the discount policy in force. The list does not include storage services, which are quoted separately.

## Calculation example
A general cargo order of 10 pallets between Montevideo and Punta del Este is quoted at the list rate multiplied by the number of pallets, plus VAT. If the cargo is refrigerated, the surcharge is applied first to the rate per pallet.
