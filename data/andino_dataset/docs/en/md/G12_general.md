
# About Andino

## Who we are

Andino is a Uruguayan technology company specializing in digital solutions for organizations. Founded in 2018, it helps businesses improve their processes through software, automation, and artificial intelligence.

## Our mission

To develop reliable and accessible technology that enables organizations to work more efficiently, make better decisions, and provide better services to their customers.

## Our vision

To become a leading technology company in Latin America, recognized for the quality of its solutions, the security of its systems, and its commitment to customers.

## What we do

Andino provides:
- Custom software development.
- Technology consulting.
- Business process automation.
- Systems integration.
- Artificial intelligence and data analytics solutions.
- Cloud infrastructure and services.

## Location and operations

Andino's headquarters are located in Montevideo, Uruguay. The company works with clients in Uruguay and other Latin American markets.

## Leadership

The executive team is led by Ramón Pereira, CEO of Andino.
